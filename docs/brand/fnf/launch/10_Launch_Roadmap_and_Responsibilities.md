# 10 — Official Launch Roadmap, Ownership & Acceptance

**Status:** Proposed sequence, not a published launch date.  
**Related:** `04_Collections_and_Real_Products.md`, `09_Website_CMS_Content_and_SEO.md`, `11_Justin_Review_Questions_and_Decisions.md`

## Principle: launch the brand, not a fake finished universe

FNF can launch with a modest inventory, a sharp identity, truthful origin, good product photos and an honest invitation. It does **not** need hundreds of SKU mockups, invented events or a high-dollar bike promotion. A deliberate small release is credible if the site actually works and visitors immediately understand what FNF stands for.

## Phase 0 — Asset and truth audit

**Goal:** Know what actually exists.  
**Checklist:** Real-stock list; photos and exact product variants; source logo bundle; shipping/returns; operating contact; founder story; rights/permissions; relevant vendor details; domain/analytics ownership.  
**Exit:** Justin has answered what is real, what is a concept and who has authority to approve publication.

## Phase 1 — A credible storefront

**Work:** Unify logo/navigation/footers, confirm a primary voice, publish fully accurate product pages, verify checkout/shipping, fix mobile issues, remove overclaims, ensure hero/collections work.  
**Exit:** A visitor can understand FNF, select an actual variant, buy successfully, access contact/returns and see the correct product details.  
**Do not delay for:** Bike wrap, many-new-design concepts or any promotion.

## Phase 2 — Brand-story lift

**Work:** Replace placeholder About text with Justin-approved version; tell the real first-batch story; publish a few product/editorial photos; create the first real community invitation and owned-email signup.  
**Exit:** Honest About, coherent identity system and at least one meaningful editorial story.

## Phase 3 — Collection organization

**Work:** Clean Essentials / High Octane / Masters logic, tag approved product artwork, introduce Flightline as a *concept gallery* if Justin wants it, implement reusable CMS sections.  
**Exit:** New ideas can be added without bespoke HTML rewrites or duplicating unrelated editors.

## Phase 4 — Bike project + creator opportunities

**Work:** Capture pickup with consent; document design/wrap progress; create project timeline on site; offer Masters824 a clean brand overview and a mutually useful conversation if appropriate.  
**Exit:** Authentic footage and credited site story, clear partnership status, no falsely implied endorsement.

## Phase 5 — Official launch moment

**Work:** A real ready-to-publish site, approved visuals, verified stock/support, announcement images and optional organic creator acknowledgements with permission.  
**Exit:** Everyone involved can truthfully describe what's on sale, what's coming, and how to contact FNF. Mark **actual launch date** only when confirmed by Justin.

## Content launch package (minimum viable)

- One 15–30 second clean FNF identity/gear montage.
- One 5–8 image product/photo carousel using actual items.
- One founder note in plain language.
- Two truthful collection introductions.
- One responsive homepage with shopping and brand story.
- One welcome email and one post-launch progress email, opt-in only.
- One short real photo/video project feature, if the relevant footage/rights exist.

## Release acceptance checklist

### Product truth
- [ ] Product titles, prices, sizes, colors and inventory checked.
- [ ] Logo placement/print materials accurately represented.
- [ ] Vendor approvals/photos available for each real SKU.
- [ ] Real orders, payments, taxes, shipping and returns tested with appropriate authorized accounts.

### Site experience
- [ ] Mobile 360/390/430 and tablet + large desktop layouts inspected.
- [ ] No blank edit or product screen, broken hero, blocked drawer or missing footer.
- [ ] Pages crawl and load; metadata and alt text present; no unsupported statistics.
- [ ] Support/policy/contact links present and functional.

### Editorial / rights
- [ ] Founder-origin claims checked.
- [ ] Real customers, collaborators and location media used only with permission.
- [ ] No unconfirmed partnership, event or high-value prize advertised.
- [ ] Draft concepts never look like fulfilled orders.

## Measures worth watching (no fabricated targets)

- Shop: sessions → product views → cart → checkout → successful orders; abandoned checkout investigation.
- Brand: traffic to About and project stories, meaningful newsletter opt-ins.
- Merch: returns/size issues, sold units by actual SKU, customer questions, print-quality issues.
- Content: completion/view-through, genuine comments, saves, inbound collaboration interest.
- Ops: upload-to-publish turnaround and percentage of images with approvals/rights.

Don't mistake social reach for genuine sales or brand trust. Set numerical goals after a real baseline exists.

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Too many parallel design directions | Choose core identity now; hold the rest as Concepts |
| Product image drift | Keep original photos and exact manufacturer/print art as authority |
| Backend/CMS drift | One typed source of truth, portable sections and clear site ownership |
| Creator dependence | FNF brand story remains compelling without a particular influencer |
| Legal promotion complexity | Keep the bike project independent; separate counsel-led review |
| Busy schedules | Audio notes, approval gates, weekly async check-in, decision log |

## A realistic two-person week

**Justin (15–25 min, split however convenient):** Record or type three answers, supply one photo batch if available, approve one visual/copy draft.  
**Sam:** Incorporate decisions, move approved assets into production, show a before/after or real page link, update the decision log.  
**Together:** No recurring meeting required unless a choice cannot be resolved asynchronously.
