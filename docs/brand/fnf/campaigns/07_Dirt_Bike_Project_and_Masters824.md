### Chapter 1 — The handover

- Justin's own simple context: why he entered, how he found out, what the pickup means (only what is true).
- Arrival establishing images.

- Both people with the stationary bike, not forced brand endorsements.

### Chapter 2 — Choosing the FNF look

- Pull up physical F&FT badge and the website identity palette.
- Try 2–3 graphical treatments in **mockups**: Heritage shield / High Octane black-red / Time/Hourglass minimal.
- Show how a design moves from rendering to shop-ready proof.
- Invite *feedback* ("Which styling fits FNF?") rather than a public promise or competition.

### Chapter 3 — Design to real object

- Meet wrap/design partner (with consent); show safe workshop footage, measuring/cleaning/proof check and final static reveal.
- Record realistic costs, timeframe, rights, credits, materials and a clean before/after.
- No footage should require unsafe vehicle maneuvers or staff to work unsafely for a camera.

### Chapter 4 — The people around the project

- Short interviews: owner/creator, designer, wrap specialist, merch maker, friends. Let each person describe a truthful detail.
- Founder reflection: why FNF is interested in machines and what the project means to the community.
- Any follow-up with Masters824 only after an explicit mutually agreeable plan.

### Chapter 5 — The full story

- Editorial feature `FNF Projects / The Bike` with chapters and timestamps.
- Licensed photos, captions, collaborator attribution, optional real related products.

## CboysTV: what to learn without copying promotions

The user highlighted CboysTV's public winner archive as compelling inspiration. The **safe, generalizable** lesson is narrative continuity: make a meaningful project introduction, show real people and evidence, document the aftermath, and keep an accessible archive. Do **not** reverse-engineer or reproduce high-value prize or purchase-entry mechanics. Any contemplated prize promotion must undergo separate adult operational and qualified legal review before it becomes a public campaign.
