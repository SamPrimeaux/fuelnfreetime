# 14 — Prompt Library for Justin's ChatGPT

**Use:** Copy a prompt into a chat inside the **shared FNF Project** and attach any relevant references. Responses are proposals until Justin approves them.  
**Related:** `03_Brand_Identity_Logos_and_Production.md`, `06_Video_Content_and_Social_Ideas.md`, `12_ChatGPT_Shared_Project_Setup.md`

## A. Founder voice: shape what the brand means

```text
Read the FNF positioning and voice docs. Interview me like a thoughtful
friend, not a branding consultant. Ask one question at a time about why
I started FNF, what I like working on, what free time means to me and
what kind of humor feels natural. After five answers, draft two 120-word
About blurbs: one heartfelt and one with garage humor. Preserve my actual
facts and mark anything you could not verify.
```

## B. Three genuinely different taglines

```text
Based on the FNF brief and my latest feedback, develop 12 headlines
across three clear categories: heritage/earned-time, garage-humor and
broad lifestyle. Make them short enough for a shirt, website hero or
packaging. Do not lift competitors' slogans. Explain where each works;
recommend only three finalists and ask me which one sounds human.
```

## C. A product design critique, not a random redesign

```text
Here is a mockup. Identify the main motif, front/back layout,
readability, likely shirt-color options, connection to FNF brand
architecture and any production problems. Preserve the original idea.
Do not invent a manufacturer-ready vector or promise perfect lettering.
Mark this as CONCEPT ONLY. Propose three targeted refinements, not a
complete style replacement.
```

## D. Remaster an actual product photo for the store

```text
Use the attached ORIGINAL physical-product photograph as ground truth.
Create a clean commercial product presentation of the SAME item.
Preserve exact product silhouette, real garment/finish color, F&FT
letterforms, chest/back print placement, real stitching, proportions,
materials visible in the source and any actual brand markings.
Improve lighting, background and framing only. Do not invent seams,
logos, features, colors, print processes, product size, or a different
physical item. Neutral premium studio background, realistic shadows,
sharp but believable fabric or material texture, 4:5 crop, no text
or watermark. If a printed detail cannot be reproduced accurately,
state the limitation and prefer an edit of the source image.
```

**Review after image creation:** Zoom into the F&FT lettering, the ampersand, border and hourglass text; compare against the actual photo. If any differ, label the result *marketing concept only*, not a faithful listing image.

## E. Premium lifestyle presentation of a real F&FT hat

```text
Using the actual F&FT cap reference, create a restrained product
campaign image with the same real cap on a tidy industrial workbench.
Natural soft window light, subdued charcoal and metal context, honest
fabric/stitch detail. Preserve exactly the cap shape, closure if visible,
patch size, badge text, patch colors and brim curvature from the photo.
No additional branding or made-up side/back embroidery. This is a
campaign visual, not a substitute for the original accurate catalog
photo. 4:5 composition.
```

## F. Actual tumbler / coffee editorial

```text
Use the attached actual F&FT black tumbler photo. Create a clean editorial
still-life next to coffee on a well-lit workshop table. Preserve the
same matte-black finish, real bands, taper, logo geometry and logo color.
No new features or invented capacity. Make the tumbler hero-sized and
photographically credible; no additional graphic/text overlay.
```

## G. Manufacturer badge-quality review

```text
Study the supplied F&FT badge original and reconstructed SVG proofs.
Make a checklist of shape fidelity, border continuity, ampersand
legibility, small negative spaces and intended one-color reproduction.
For laser engraving, embroidery and print, give the supplier questions
we must answer. Do NOT claim the file is production-approved. Propose a
proof plan with physical sizes and vendor constraints left blank for
our manufacturer to specify.
```

## H. FNF collection concept board

```text
Take these four aviation T-shirt mockups and create a written visual
brief for a proposed 'Flightline' collection. Preserve what is unique
about each design. Organize by front mark, back hero art, garment color,
headline, accent palette and manufacturing dependencies. This is a
creative concept board, not real inventory or authorized print artwork.
Suggest page presentation modules that the existing CMS can reuse.
```

## I. Real short-form video from an unscripted shoot

```text
These are my actual clips/notes. Give me three edit structures suitable
for a 20–45 second Reel or Short. I don't want canned scripts: use
natural audio where possible. Recommend a first three-second visual,
clip order, a factual on-screen title, one short caption and an ending.
Only use footage I supplied or facts I confirm. Flag missing media,
creator permissions and any sensitive footage.
```

## J. Plan pickup-day footage without performance pressure

```text
Read the dirt-bike project and video docs. I'm going to pick up the
bike from Masters824. Give me a single-screen checklist of safe,
stationary footage, conversation starters and still photographs.
Prioritize the natural meeting, the bike detail, a two-person photo if
permitted, safe loading, and my honest reflection. Don't assume a formal
partnership or ask anyone to perform dangerous maneuvers.
```

## K. Creator collaboration one-page brief

```text
Draft a one-page non-presumptuous FNF creative introduction for a
potential collaborator. Show our actual existing products, philosophy,
site and a possible build-story angle. Distinguish what is real from
ideas we could explore together. Make it beneficial to both sides but
don't promise exposure, sponsorship, compensation or product rights.
Add a short permissions/credits/rights checklist.
```

## L. A better About page using my real story

```text
Use ONLY my approved founder notes and the FNF source docs. Write an
About page: hook, 150-word story, the idea behind the hourglass,
what real merchandise we're making, a community invitation and two CTAs.
Don't manufacture a founding day, city detail or supplier story. Offer
two tone options: understated and moderately irreverent.
```

## M. Product listing from confirmed facts

```text
Create a clean product title, description, key details, care section,
size/fit guidance placeholder, alt-text captions and SEO title for this
F&FT product. These are the ONLY verified facts: [paste details].
Leave unknown fields as NEEDS CONFIRMATION rather than guessing. Tone:
conversational, confident, not overly hyped.
```

## N. One week's useful content from one shoot

```text
I only have 20 minutes of footage from [real activity]. Plan a
sustainable batch: one short video, a photo carousel, a concise brand
story and one email paragraph. Each must convey a distinct part of the
same real experience. Identify what to photograph/crop and any content
rights still needed. Do not manufacture footage or claim partnerships.
```

## O. Justin's weekly inbox in five minutes

```text
Review the latest conversations and documents in this FNF Project.
Tell me (1) the three biggest decisions waiting for me, (2) what's
already approved with evidence, (3) what Sam can complete without me,
and (4) anything being represented as fact that still needs checking.
Make it short. Ask me only one question at a time after the summary.
```

## P. Sam's end-of-week implementation handoff

```text
Using the approved FNF decisions and the latest site/CMS discussion,
write a release-oriented task brief. Include goal, current evidence,
files/content/resources affected, acceptance tests, permission needs,
launch blockers and what must NOT be inferred. Avoid creating another
parallel product gallery, CMS editor or site-specific workaround.
```

## Q. Turn a messy voice note into usable brand input

```text
Below is a rough voice-note transcript from Justin. Preserve his tone
and meaning. Return: EXACT INTENT (plain English), FACTS, STRONG
PREFERENCES, IDEAS NOT APPROVED, QUESTIONS FOR SAM, and up to three
next actions. Do not interpret jokes as instructions or concepts as
product promises. Quote only short phrases needed for accuracy.
```

## R. Distill a brand photo batch

```text
I'm sharing 12 mixed FNF pictures. Give each a short temporary asset ID,
classify as EXISTING PRODUCT / DESIGN CONCEPT / APPROVED LOGO / OTHER
REFERENCE / STORY PHOTO, suggest tags and potential page placements,
and list the missing information needed for safe publication. Do not
assert licensing or current inventory from appearance alone.
```

## Prompt craft shortcut

For any request, include: **What this is for → exact reference → what must stay unchanged → desired finish → known facts → unknowns → approval stage.** You will get more useful drafts and fewer convincing-but-wrong mockups.
