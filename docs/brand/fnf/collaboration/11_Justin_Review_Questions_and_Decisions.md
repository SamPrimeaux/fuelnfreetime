## The founder's voice — ask Justin, don't invent answers

- What is the **true reason** you came up with FNF?
- When did this idea become serious enough to have sample products made?
- Who did you first tell about it, and what was their reaction?
- What does "free time" mean personally to you right now?
- What's a story from a garage, boat, drive, trip or ordinary weekend that people will understand?
- What kind of person do you hope wears the badge?
- Is there anything you absolutely **do not** want the brand to represent?

These are questions to spark genuine history, not requests for grand cinematic mythology.

## Visual design questions

- F&FT: primary company logo or heritage apparel badge?
- Official brand spelling: `Fuel N Free Time` / `Fuel & Free Time` / other?
- Favorite pair: black/ivory, black/red/white, black/gold, or separate by collection?
- Do the historic/gold shield variants accurately reflect what was actually made?
- Which marks do you want on the neck tag, cap, tumbler, front chest, back print, website?
- Which of Smoke Show / Wingman / Low Pass Legend would you actually want someone to make first?

## Merch questions

- Do you prefer clean small chest logos, giant illustrated backs, or both?
- Which blanks/vendors/decoration methods did you use for the first items?
- What size chart do we trust and what did you learn from the samples?
- What manufacturing quality would embarrass you if a customer received it?
- Are there products that are **for display only** and shouldn't be sold yet?

## Video & bike questions

- What part of the bike pickup are you most excited about?
- Is Masters824 already aware of the idea of filming a brand story? What permission exists?
- Is the bike wrap a confirmed plan, or a concept needing quotes and design approval?
- Do you prefer to be on camera, film more POV/voice notes, or let candid moments carry the story?
- Is there a local maker, builder or friend you'd want to spotlight next?

## How to review without needing a meeting

Reply in a ChatGPT Project chat with a shorthand like:

```text
FNF REVIEW — [date]
KEEP: black-and-gold badge on hats; honest garage voice
CHANGE: less inspirational copy; more attitude
MAYBE: Flightline as a limited series later
FACT CHECK: I need to confirm product materials with vendor
NOT APPROVED: giveaway/promo copy
NEXT THING I CAN SEND: one real sample photo + 1-minute voice note
```

If Justin sends a voice note, ChatGPT can summarize it into these categories. **Both people should review the resulting summary** for errors before treating it as a decision.

| Date | Topic | Decision | Status | Decider | Evidence / link | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD | Official name | Pending | OPEN | Justin | — | Confirm spelling |
| YYYY-MM-DD | F&FT badge | Preserve original form, clean production treatment | PROPOSED | Justin | Proof sheet | Review and approve |

| YYYY-MM-DD | Official launch | Date undecided | OPEN | Justin + Sam | QA checklist | Test site |

**Status vocabulary:** IDEA → IN REVIEW → APPROVED / REJECTED / PARKED. For factual issues use NEEDS EVIDENCE. An AI-generated suggestion must never silently flip to APPROVED.
