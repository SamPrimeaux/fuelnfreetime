# A note to Justin — FNF brand ideas + an easier way for us to collaborate

Hey Justin —

I've been pulling together the photos, mockups, old brand notes and product samples you've shared, along with some research into apparel/lifestyle brands that seem like they could resonate with the direction you're building.

**The big idea I keep coming back to is that FNF is about time, not just motorsports.** Bikes, cars, garages, boats and all the things people are passionate about are the *fuel*. The point is getting to enjoy those hours. I think there's a real identity there, with room for good apparel, a little attitude, community stories, and all the projects you want to get into.

I put the work into a few separate docs so you don't have to read one gigantic conversation. There's a brand direction, some manifesto and tagline drafts, logo/manufacturing notes, collection ideas, video concepts for the bike pickup and regular FNF content, examples of other brands to look at, and a site/launch plan. **None of it is meant to lock you into a brand voice that isn't yours.** Circle the stuff you like, tell me what feels corny, and send any random idea when it hits you.

I also included instructions for making a **shared ChatGPT Project**. That way you and I can drop in photos, voice notes and new ideas whenever we have a minute. Our chats can build on the same reference files, and we can keep a quick running list of what you actually approve versus what we're still brainstorming. No need for us to be available at the same time.

**You can start with just three things:**

1. Which tagline feels the most like FNF (or none of them)?
2. How edgy/garage-humor do you want the brand to be?
3. What shirts, hats or other items are actually ready to sell right now?

That's enough for me to keep getting the site and product presentations moving while we figure out the finer details. The goal isn't a fake glossy brand—it's to make *your* ideas look and feel as strong as they deserve.

— Sam
