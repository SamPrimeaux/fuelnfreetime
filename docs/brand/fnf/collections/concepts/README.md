## How to add a design

1. Preserve Justin's supplied mockup or original art as-is and record the source.
2. Copy [Asset Record](../../creative/templates/ASSET_RECORD.md) into this folder with a stable design ID (example: `fnf-flightline-smoke-show-a.md`).
3. Record front/back placements, intended garment, colorways, typography, and feedback.
4. Link **actual production artwork** only when obtained/created and rights verified.
5. Do not invent products, stock, manufacturing readiness or creator endorsements.

**Publishing:** Concept boards may appear in clearly labeled editorial previews *after review*. Actual inventory and product choices remain with FNF Product Studio and commerce records. Do not hardcode another products list here.
