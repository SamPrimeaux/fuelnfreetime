## Intended applications

- Apparel and garment printing
- Embroidery preparation
- Laser engraving and etching
- Stickers, patches and accessories
- Website and promotional graphics

## Important

These files were reconstructed from supplied visual references.

They are not certified production masters.

Confirm exact brand spelling, artwork geometry,
minimum line thickness, colors and material requirements.

Require a physical manufacturer proof before bulk production.

Keep original references and all future approved versions.
Do not silently overwrite an approved master.

## Preview gallery

Six PNG previews are uploaded to the existing [FNF Content library](https://fuelnfreetime.com/admin/content), under the **FNF Identity — Review v1** album. The album is useful for creative review and selecting artwork in Product Studio; it is **not** a certification of logo geometry, print suitability, or manufacturer acceptance. This repository remains the source for the original archive and master files. See `docs/brand/fnf/creative/asset-registry.json` and `scripts/import-fnf-brand-previews.mjs` for repeatable identifiers.
