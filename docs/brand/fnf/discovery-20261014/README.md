## Objective
Establish a recognizable Fuel N Free Time lifestyle identity for people who work hard and make time for machines, crafts, family, friends and their passions; do not reduce the brand to generic motorsport apparel or repeatedly invent new logos.

- October 14 user photos: studio-style black graphic tee (gold F&FT chest, fuel/hourglass illustrated back, “Earn Your Freedom”) and real-world photo of three dark caps with pale F&FT marks. The studio depiction is not proof of a particular stocked SKU; cap photo is not proof of quantities or vendor techniques.

## Finding: continuity already exists
The F&FT badge already appears on caps and the tee chest. The hourglass expresses the premium dimension of time, while a larger back graphic shows the expressive/art-driven side. Rather than replacing those, separate roles: primary identity, one-ink physical decoration, expressive shirt graphics, site wordmark, and responsive favicon.

## Five unresolved identity conflicts
1. **Naming:** Fuel N Free Time vs Fuel & Free Time; FNF as abbreviation; F&FT as badge.
2. **Master geometry:** exact source/creator/version of official F&FT logo not verified.
3. **Palette:** existing red-on-black artwork, gold-on-black graphic tee, light monochrome cap embroidery/patch. Color roles not finalized.
4. **Typography:** classic badge serif differs from historical `Bebas Neue`/Inter prefill; do not substitute a font into the actual mark.
5. **Evidence:** photos and concepts are not permission to claim stock, materials, manufacturing origin, exclusivity or partnerships.
