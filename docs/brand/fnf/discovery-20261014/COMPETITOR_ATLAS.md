# Fuel N Free Time — Competitor & Adjacent-Brand Study
**Researched October 14, 2026.** Original analysis of official public brand descriptions. Inspiration ≠ affiliation, permission or comparative sales validation.

| Brand & site | What it does well | What FNF should study (not imitate) |
|---|---|---|
| **Fasthouse** — https://www.fasthouse.com/pages/about-us | Garage and track culture, recognizable core identity, shared community and casual apparel. | Brand symbol consistency on goods; real people and events; approachable garage credibility. Avoid copycat racing identity. |
| **Iron & Resin** — https://ironandresin.com/pages/about | Craft, real small-company history, purposeful garments, restrained editorial photography. | Founder/product story, texture and material detail, shop photography, honest manufacturing descriptions. Don't borrow their origin or performance guarantees. |
| **Dixxon** — https://www.dixxon.com/pages/about-us | Focused signature apparel, distinctive physical products and deliberate collections. | Make first real F&FT hat/tee recognizable; consistent photo angles and detail shots. Do not invent scarcity or quality claims. |
| **Deus Ex Machina** — https://deuscustoms.com/pages/about | A shared maker/creative philosophy unites motors, surfing, art and everyday culture. | A wider cultural umbrella: enthusiasts and their interests are the story, not just specific vehicles. Do not copy Deus' visual language. |
| **Howler Brothers** — https://howlerbros.com/pages/about-howler-brothers | Fun, human identity spanning outdoors, music and apparel; repeatable recognizable product treatments. | Personality without forced toughness; original designs tied to the community. Do not borrow its western/surf motifs. |
| **Roark** — https://roark.com/en-ca/pages/about-roark | Collection-level narrative and field reporting connect garments with travel stories. | Real project journals and maker portraits could lead to limited *truthful* capsules. Never fabricate trip or creator lore. |

## What to compare in the browser
Capture and annotate each brand's favicon at 16px, mobile homepage opening, menu structure, apparel category grid, product detail photography/variants, About or journal, and how the primary mark appears on a *real item*. Log URL/date and one specific learning per reference; avoid generic “premium” votes.

## Hypothesized whitespace for FNF
**Time first, fuel second.** Racing clothing tells you the activity, heritage workwear often tells you the material, destination apparel tells you the place. FNF could distinguish itself with the **earned freedom** between work and meaningful time with machines, craft and people. This claim needs customer/founder validation.

## Application concept (not approved)
- Show a real cap or chest-badge tee on the first mobile screen, with clear product information.
- Use a restrained signature mark as the quiet identity and a bigger original illustration on selected back prints.
- Build supported shop categories: Essentials (approved real goods), High Octane (approved graphic work), Masters (real experience/skill features); Flightline remains conceptual.
- Include real behind-the-scenes stories and honest size, inventory, fabrication, returns and image metadata.
- Mobile-first editorial layout, intentional hierarchy, no artificial hype, no large generic cards dominating the page.
