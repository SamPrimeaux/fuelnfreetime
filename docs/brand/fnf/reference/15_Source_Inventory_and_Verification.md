## F. Naming / rights / manufacturing approval gates

1. **Brand naming:** Confirm official public/business wording and logo display rules.
2. **Source art:** Clarify who owns/created each mockup, whether third-party aviation/vehicle imagery or fonts are involved, and whether commercial merchandise rights exist.
3. **Master art:** Compare cleaned vector paths against approved identity; test physical reproduction in actual sizes/materials.
4. **Product truth:** Confirm physical stock, descriptions, dimensions, pricing, manufacturing claims and photos.
5. **Creator content:** Permission to capture and use likeness/audio; separately obtain rights if ads/paid endorsements are involved.
6. **Website content:** Confirm founders' stories, event dates, reviews, quantities and operational details before launch.
7. **Promotions:** Any valuable-prize campaign is separate and subject to responsible adult business/legal review. This kit contains no entry mechanic or purchase-linked prize strategy.
8. **Collaboration:** Only approvals recorded explicitly by Justin count as brand decisions.
