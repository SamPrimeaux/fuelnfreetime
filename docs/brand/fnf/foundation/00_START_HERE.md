## Quick read: the brand in 60 seconds

**Fuel is what moves you. Free time is what you live for.**

The most promising direction is a recognizable apparel and lifestyle identity centered on **time**: the hours people deliberately make for vehicles, travel, projects, friends, coffee, family, hobbies, and the pursuit of a good life. Engines are a natural and visually exciting expression of that idea, not a requirement for belonging.

The vibe: an actual person from the garage who can laugh at himself, knows good gear, enjoys talking with people from different generations, and cares more about how you spend your weekend than impressing strangers. Premium execution, unpretentious conversation, a little attitude.

**Working headline:** *The best hours aren't on the clock.*  
**Supporting phrase already seen on product:** *Earn Your Freedom.*  
**Proposed punchier campaign voice:** *Clock out. Fire up.*

Nothing here overrides Justin's taste. Everything marked *proposed* can be changed without dismantling the site's CMS or product structure.

## Minimal setup (10 minutes, even on a busy day)

1. Read **this** and `01` + `11`; reply to only **three** decisions: favorite tagline, preferred amount of attitude, and which existing merch is actually available.
2. One person creates **one shared ChatGPT Project** named `Fuel N Free Time — Brand & Builds`; steps are in `12`.
3. Upload the Markdown files individually into the project (don't assume a ZIP is parsed). Add a small selection of the original photos and the logo proof sheet. Keep the original master ZIP in a separate permanent file location.
4. Invite the other person with **Edit** access (private invitation, not public link).
5. Put each subject into a separate project chat: `BRAND`, `PRODUCTS`, `BIKE STORY`, `CONTENT`, `SITE`, `DECISIONS`. You don't need everyone present at once.
6. Each week create one `WEEKLY DECISIONS` chat: what changed, what's approved, what's blocked, and next actions.

## Shared rules

- **Approved:** Explicitly approved by Justin and recorded in the decision log.
- **Working:** Good candidate but still open for iteration.
- **Concept only:** Not a real stocked item, contracted partner, scheduled event, or published campaign.
- **Needs verification:** A public claim requiring evidence or a manufacturing/permissions detail still unknown.
- **Do not fake:** Origin stories, review quotes, memberships, product attributes, stock, partnerships, event counts, safety claims, discount claims or provenance.
- **Physical products:** Accurate product photography must be distinguishable from conceptual AI-generated campaign art.
- **Master art:** Treat vector reconstructions as **vendor-review files**, not automatically print/engrave/embroider-approved masters.
- **Giveaway:** No public entry/purchase/prize promotion mechanism until appropriate adult operators and qualified legal advisors review it. It is separate from the brand launch.

## The three things we're trying to achieve

1. **Authenticity:** Build around Justin's actual experiences, merchandise, humor, communities, and opinions rather than inventing a brand persona for him.
2. **Presentation:** Give actual merch the quality of imagery, product pages, and storytelling that makes the brand look legitimate.
3. **Reuse:** Store brand, product, artwork, collection, campaign and story information in a usable CMS so it doesn't become another static pile of pages and mockups.
