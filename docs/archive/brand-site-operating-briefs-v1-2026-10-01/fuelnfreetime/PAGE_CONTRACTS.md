# Fuel & Free Time — Page Contracts v1

## Home
**Job:** Explain the belief, establish visual world, route to products/story/community.  
**Primary CTA:** Shop/explore current collections.  
**Secondary:** Learn the story / join list.  
**Must not:** become Shop duplicated at `/`.

## Shop
**Job:** Convert brand interest into product discovery and purchase.  
**Primary CTA:** Choose product/options.  
**Must include:** collection navigation, current products, loading/error/empty states, lifestyle bridge.  
**Reusable extraction:** light/frosted header preset, collection cards, product-grid shell.

## About
**Job:** Explain why this brand exists and make “time is the real horsepower” mean something.  
**Structure:** thesis → Fuel → Free Time → origin → values/philosophy → people/lifestyle → CTA.  
**Reusable extraction:** story hero, manifesto, timeline/story-media, value cards, CTA band.

## Community
**Job:** Turn the lifestyle claim into genuine participation and proof.  
**Structure:** manifesto → real gallery/stories → upcoming events → past events → submission/collaboration → join list.  
**Empty state:** honest “we’re planning the first…” copy instead of fake stats/events.

## Collaborate
**Job:** Qualify and capture partnership opportunities.  
**Structure:** fit → collaboration types → examples → form → what happens next.

## Cart
**Job:** reassure, adjust order, apply valid discount, capture required identity, start secure payment.  
**Must verify:** quantity/remove/discount/shipping threshold/email/payment errors/success/mobile.

## Policies / Terms
**Job:** provide clear operational/legal trust.  
**Must verify:** business identity, effective date, support contact, shipping, returns/refunds, privacy, terms.

## Admin Home
**Job:** operational “what needs attention today?” dashboard, not decoration.

## Orders
**Job:** find, inspect, act on, and audit order/fulfillment/payment state.

## Product Create
**Job:** go from provider/catalog idea → artwork → variant → preview → pricing → publish.

## Artwork Help
**Job:** teach valid art inputs and prevent production errors before creation.

## Products
**Job:** browse/edit/publish/archive products with media, status, inventory and provider truth.

## Inventory
**Job:** know sellable state and prevent oversell/false availability.

## Subscribers
**Job:** audience list, consent/source, segmentation, engagement, export/actions.

## Growth
**Job:** create campaigns, transform store/content signals into channel drafts, and measure outcomes.

## Discounts
**Job:** define valid offers with guardrails, usage visibility, and expiration.

## Content
**Job:** durable media/content library with albums, optimization, SEO, usage, versions and publishing destinations.

## Markets
**Status:** PARK until a concrete multi-region/catalog/currency/channel requirement exists.

## Analytics Overview / Finance / Health
**Job:** trusted decision surfaces. Every metric needs source, as-of, coverage, empty/error state and drill-down.

## Online Store
**Job:** store status, theme/version, performance, preview, edit/publish. Fix typed metric rendering.

## Theme Editor
**Job:** visual composition of sections/blocks/media/layout. Must stay synchronized with selected page and canonical page content.

## Page Editor
**Job:** structured content, page visibility and search metadata. Must share the same underlying document as Theme Editor.

## Preferences
**Job:** store-level access, identity, social/SEO defaults, navigation/global configuration.

## POS
**Status:** PARK until F&FT actually needs an in-person sales workflow.

## AgentSam
**Job:** contextual assistant/tooling layer for operator tasks; should act on the same contracts as the admin rather than invent parallel state.

## Email
**Job (future):** operational shared mailbox with provider sync, templates, threading, permissions, audit and automation.
