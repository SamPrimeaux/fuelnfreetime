## Creative approach

- **Off Clock** — everyday line: tachometer becomes a timepiece, because time is the real horsepower.
- **First Light** — water and wild: launch before sunrise, warm solar arc, shoreline/wake geometry.
- **Shop Notes** — garage culture: workshop stamp and mechanical plate, made for the people who keep building.

The shared capsule and campaign framing are **proposals**. Justin's working brand dossier remains canonical; no pending idea becomes a confirmed brand promise by being included here.

## Before moving any item from draft to active

1. Justin reviews visual direction, words, products and color choices; approve or request revisions.
2. Replace illustrated garment cover with a verified front/back supplier mockup. Keep concept art as separate versioned source.
3. Select actual Completeful blank, production profile and valid multi-size/multi-color variants; prove provider mapping and purchase/fulfillment authority.
4. Add verified material, sizing/fit, price/margin, shipping and care details. Current price 0 is a **draft placeholder**; do not publish as free merchandise.
5. Prove save/reload, image gallery, checkout availability and mobile PDP in staging before deliberate publication.

No automated activation, checkout, provider order, database migration, or live theme deployment is part of this concept pass.
